Risk Notice

Important risks related to self-custody wallets, blockchain transactions and digital asset markets.

PrivacyTermsCookiesSupport

Last updated: May 13, 2026

Digital asset volatility

Digital assets can be highly volatile. Prices may rise or fall rapidly and may become illiquid. You can lose some or all of the value associated with digital assets.

Self-custody risk

Altbase Wallet is designed for self-custody. You are responsible for your backup phrase, private keys, passwords and device security. If recovery information is lost, funds may be permanently inaccessible.

Irreversible transactions

Blockchain transactions are generally irreversible. If you send funds to the wrong address, use the wrong network or approve an unintended transaction, recovery may be impossible.

Security threats

Phishing websites, malware, fake support accounts, malicious downloads and clipboard replacement attacks can lead to loss of funds. Always verify URLs, checksums, addresses and support contacts.

Market data risk

Prices shown on the website are informational and may be delayed, unavailable or inaccurate. Portfolio values are estimates and should not be treated as execution prices or financial advice.

Software and network risk

Wallet software, operating systems, blockchains, nodes and third-party infrastructure can contain bugs, downtime or security vulnerabilities. Keep software updated and maintain secure backups.

Regulatory and tax risk

Digital asset rules vary by jurisdiction and may change. You are responsible for understanding legal, tax and reporting obligations that may apply to you.

No guarantee

Altbase Wallet does not guarantee asset value, market availability, continuous access, transaction confirmation times or recovery of lost funds.
