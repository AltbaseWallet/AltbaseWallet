What cookies and local storage are

Cookies and local storage are small pieces of data stored by your browser. They help websites remember preferences and keep features working between page loads.

Essential storage

Altbase Wallet may use essential local storage or cookies to remember your theme preference, cookie notice choice and basic interface settings. These are used to make the site function properly.
